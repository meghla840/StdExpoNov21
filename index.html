What to bring

  • Passport, if you have one
  • SSC, HSC or degree certificates and transcripts
  • IELTS or other English test score, if you have one
  • Your slip (printed or on your phone)

Questions worth asking

  • Which intake is best for my result and budget?
  • What will the total cost be, including living costs?
  • What is the deadline for each scholarship?
  • What documents do I need for the visa application?

Why a student visa may be rejected

Reason 1Fake or mismatched documentsFalse or mismatched information can lead to rejection or a future ban.
Reason 2Not enough fundsYou cannot cover your costs, or cannot explain where your funds came from.
Reason 3Course does not match your studiesYou cannot explain why this course and this country.
Reason 4Unclear study planYour plan after study is unclear, or it looks like you only want to work.
Reason 5Hiding a past refusalAlways declare earlier refusals. Hiding them makes things worse.
Reason 6Genuine intention not clearThe embassy is not convinced that your main purpose is to study.
